Culture & Values

What Limo stands for.

Our Principles

  • 🎯 Craft over speed — We take time to build things properly.
  • 🤝 Radical transparency — Everyone knows what's happening and why.
  • 🌍 Global by default — We design for all languages, cultures, and contexts.
  • 🌱 Sustainable pace — No burnout. Sustainable work brings lasting results.
  • 💬 Async first — We document before we call. Meetings have a purpose.

Working Norms

  • All decisions get logged in the Decisions Log.
  • Every meeting needs an agenda shared 24h in advance.
  • Action items must have an owner and a due date.
  • Weekly summaries are written every Sunday by the director.
  • Feedback is given kindly, directly, and in private first.
